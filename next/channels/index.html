Your rules

You control both channels from your dashboard.

Same words, same time

Each alert is written once and published to every member who holds AAPL at the same time. Delivery speed depends on carriers and email providers.

Quiet nights by default

Overnight texts (9 PM–7 AM) are off unless you turn them on. Texts held overnight go out at 7:00 AM; overnight emails still arrive as they happen, and your 5:00 AM email sums up the night.

Stop any time

Reply STOP to end texts instantly, or switch texts or emails off in one click.
