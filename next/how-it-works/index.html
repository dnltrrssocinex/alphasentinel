How it works · We watch. We alert. You decide.

You can’t watch your stocks all day. Here’s how Alpha Sentinel does it for you.

Every trading day a rules-based screen reads five kinds of conditions: trend, momentum, volatility, volume and strength versus the S&P 500. When they turn down on a stock you hold, you get a sell alert: where to consider selling, where it could fall to, and when the call is wrong.
