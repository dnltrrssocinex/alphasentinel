02 · What it does

Three jobs. Nothing else.

01

Watches only what you own.

  • Add your holdings once.
  • Checked every session we cover, after hours included.
  • Most days: nothing to report.
1. Add the stocks you hold. 2. Alpha Sentinel checks them whenever the NYSE and Nasdaq are trading — pre-market, regular hours and after hours. 3. If one turns down: a 5:00 AM email, then a text and email when a level is reached. How it works →
02

Says where to sell — and where we’re wrong.

  • Sell zone: where to consider selling.
  • Targets: how far it could fall. Not buy signals.
  • Stop: where we’re wrong. The alert closes within 10 trading days.
Targets are where we think the stock could fall. Whether and when you buy back is up to you. If the price rises above the stop, the alert closes. Once Target 1 is reached, the stop moves to 1% above Target 1 — so the move already made isn’t handed back. Targets are our view of what might happen — not promises. You can act, or exit, at any time. A 10-day sell alert isn’t a long-term call. A stock can recover after an alert closes, and we don’t send re-entry signals. See a real alert →
03

Shows every result. Misses included.

  • Free lookup on any stock.
  • Same rules for every stock and member.
  • Nothing edited or deleted.
Win — stock fell more than 1% from the sell price. Loss — rose more than 1%. Draw — in between. Grades score the alert, not anyone’s return. Win rates show after 20 closed alerts. Read the Record →
