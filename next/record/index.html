Rule v1.2. Only trades after an alert is published count. The alert starts at the first trade at or inside its sell zone — including a gap straight below it — and that price is the alert’s sell price. If the price never comes down to the zone by the close of the second trading day, the alert is marked Expired, listed, and not graded. The alert then closes at the first of: Target 2 reached · the price rising above the stop (once Target 1 is reached, the stop moves to 1% above Target 1) · the 4:00 PM close of the 10th trading day after it started. Gaps close at the first trade when trading resumes. Grades: Win if the stock fell more than 1% from the sell price, Loss if it rose more than 1%, Draw in between — they score the alert, not anyone’s investment return. Each alert’s full text is fingerprinted (SHA-256) the moment it’s published, so anyone can check that a later edit matches. Corrections are logged publicly and never overwrite the original.
